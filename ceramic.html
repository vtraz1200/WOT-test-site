Ceramic Coating

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer applied by hand that chemically bonds with your vehicle's paint, forming a hard, glossy layer of protection that far outlasts traditional wax or sealant.

  • Multi-Stage Paint Correction

    Advanced machine polishing removes swirl marks, light scratches, and imperfections to restore a deep, mirror-like finish before the coating is applied.

  • Superior Hydrophobic Protection

    Water, dirt, and contaminants slide off effortlessly — making washing faster and keeping your vehicle cleaner longer.

  • Complete Surface Coverage

    Coating applied to paint, wheels, trim, and exposed surfaces for full-vehicle protection and a uniform finish.

Marine Ceramic Coating

Price varies · Priced per foot

Marine ceramic coating is a liquid polymer applied by hand that chemically bonds with your boat's gelcoat, forming a hard, glossy layer of protection that resists UV oxidation, salt, and water spotting far longer than traditional marine wax or sealant.

  • Gelcoat Correction & Oxidation Removal

    Machine compounding and polishing cut through chalky oxidation, water spotting, and light scratches to restore a deep, wet-look shine before the coating is applied.

  • Superior Hydrophobic Protection

    Water, salt, and grime sheet off the hull effortlessly — slowing scum-line buildup and making wash-downs at the dock far quicker.

  • Full Hull & Topside Coverage

    Coating applied to the hull, topsides, hardtop, and exposed metal for uniform gloss and protection above the waterline.

Final price depends on boat length and the level of gelcoat correction required — an in-person inspection is required for an accurate quote.

Regular includes a 4-year warranty; the 8-year warranty option is our Premium tier.

Please allow a 2–3 day minimum turnaround.
