PPF for Protection and Style

Self-Healing Technology

Minor scratches and swirl marks don’t have to be permanent. Advanced self-healing film technology allows light imperfections to disappear with heat from the sun or engine warmth. Your surface stays smooth and flawless over time.

UV & Fade Resistance

Constant sun exposure can cause paint to fade and oxidize. PPF blocks harmful UV rays to preserve your vehicle’s original color and gloss. It keeps your finish looking newer for longer.

Hydrophobic Surface

The film creates a slick, water-repelling surface that helps resist dirt, grime, and road contaminants. Water beads up and rolls off easily, making maintenance faster and easier. Your vehicle stays cleaner between washes.

Rock Chip & Impact Protection

PPF acts as a durable barrier between your paint and the road. It absorbs the impact from rocks, debris, and daily driving hazards before they can damage your finish. Your factory paint stays protected where it matters most.

Common Questions

PPF FAQ

How long does paint protection film last?
Quality XPEL film typically lasts 7–10 years with normal care, and carries a manufacturer warranty against yellowing, cracking, and delamination. [confirm exact warranty term]
Will it yellow or discolor over time?
Not with current XPEL film — it has UV inhibitors that keep it clear. Yellowing was a problem with older, cheaper films and is covered by the warranty on the film we install.
Can PPF be removed later?
Yes. Installed and removed correctly, it comes off cleanly without harming your factory paint — which is exactly why it protects resale value.
Full front end or full vehicle — which do I need?
If your main concern is highway rock chips, the full front end covers the panels that take almost all the damage. Full vehicle makes sense for a new or high-value car you plan to keep, or for soft/delicate paint. We’ll walk you through it.
I already have a few rock chips — can you still wrap it?
Usually yes. We assess the paint first; minor chips can be touched in before film goes on so they’re not sealed under it.
Do you offer financing?
