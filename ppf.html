PPF for Protection and Style

Self-Healing Technology

Minor scratches and swirl marks don’t have to be permanent. Advanced self-healing film technology allows light imperfections to disappear with heat from the sun or engine warmth. Your surface stays smooth and flawless over time.

UV & Fade Resistance

Constant sun exposure can cause paint to fade and oxidize. PPF blocks harmful UV rays to preserve your vehicle’s original color and gloss. It keeps your finish looking newer for longer.e.

Hydrophobic Surface

The film creates a slick, water-repelling surface that helps resist dirt, grime, and road contaminants. Water beads up and rolls off easily, making maintenance faster and easier. Your vehicle stays cleaner between washes.

Rock Chip & Impact Protection

PPF acts as a durable barrier between your paint and the road. It absorbs the impact from rocks, debris, and daily driving hazards before they can damage your finish. Your factory paint stays protected where it matters most.
