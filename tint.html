Window Tinting

Dyed Window Tint

Affordable Style & Everyday Privacy

Dyed window tint is a cost-effective solution designed to enhance your vehicle’s appearance while adding privacy and reducing glare. This film uses a dyed layer to darken your windows, giving your car a sleek, finished look.

While it improves comfort by cutting down sunlight and glare, dyed tint primarily focuses on aesthetics rather than heat rejection. It’s a great entry-level option for drivers who want a darker look without the higher price point.

Best for:

Drivers looking for an affordable upgrade with improved style and privacy.

Nano-Ceramic Window Tint

Maximum Heat Rejection. Premium Performance.

Nano-ceramic tint is the top-tier choice for drivers who want more than just a darker look. Engineered with advanced ceramic particles, this film blocks a significant amount of heat, harmful UV rays, and infrared light—keeping your vehicle cooler and more comfortable.

Unlike traditional tint, ceramic film won’t fade over time and maintains crystal-clear visibility from the inside, even at darker shades. It also won’t interfere with signals like GPS, radio, or mobile devices.

Best for:

Drivers who want superior heat rejection, long-lasting performance, and a premium driving experience.
