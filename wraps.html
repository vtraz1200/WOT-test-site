Vinyl Wraps

A vehicle wrap is a vinyl film applied to a vehicle’s painted surfaces to change its appearance and/or add protection without permanently altering the factory paint.

The material is a conformable, adhesive-backed film (typically cast vinyl) that is professionally installed over exterior panels. It can be used for:

  • Full color changes
  • Partial accents on roof wraps
  • Commercial branding and graphics
  • Paint protection applications
