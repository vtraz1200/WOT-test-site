Vinyl Wraps

What Is a Vehicle Wrap?

A vehicle wrap is a conformable, adhesive-backed vinyl film professionally installed over your vehicle's painted surfaces. It changes the vehicle's appearance and adds a layer of protection — without permanently altering the factory paint underneath.

Understanding Your Quote

What goes into your wrap price

Every wrap is quoted to the vehicle and the look you want. Four things move the number.

  • Coverage

    A roof or accent piece, a partial wrap, or a full color change — how much of the vehicle gets vinyl is the main driver.

    Accent → partial → full color change

  • Vehicle size & complexity

    A coupe wraps faster than a truck, van, or SUV with bumpers, flares, and deep recesses that all need vinyl tucked in.

    Coupe → truck, van or SUV

  • Finish

    Standard gloss and satin cost less than color-shift, chrome, textured, or printed films.

    Gloss / satin → color-shift, chrome, printed

  • Prep & extras

    Removing an old wrap, disassembling trim, or fixing paint underneath adds time before the new vinyl goes on.

    Add for old-wrap removal or prep

Why a cheaper quote usually isn’t the same job

  • Panels and trim removed so vinyl wraps into the edges, not over them
  • Post-heated so it holds through Michigan winters
  • Quality cast film that removes cleanly years later
  • Paint checked and protected underneath — not wrapped over damage

Common Questions

Wrap FAQ

How long does a wrap last?
A quality color-change wrap lasts about 5–7 years on a garage-kept vehicle. Sun and weather exposure shorten that; commercial graphics are often refreshed sooner as branding changes.
Will it damage my factory paint?
No — on factory paint in good condition, the film removes cleanly and protects the paint underneath from UV and light abrasion while it’s on. Aftermarket repaint or peeling clear-coat is a different story; we check first.
Can I wash a wrapped vehicle?
Yes — hand wash or touchless. Avoid brush car washes and pressure washing seams. We’ll give you a care sheet.
How long does the install take?
A full color change is typically a few days — the vehicle is disassembled at the edges, wrapped, and reassembled so there are no lifting edges. Partial wraps are faster.
Do you do commercial fleet graphics?
Yes — single vehicles through multi-vehicle fleets, with a repeatable spec so every unit matches. See the Fleet & Commercial section above.
