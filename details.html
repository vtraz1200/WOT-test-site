Common Questions

Detailing FAQ

How long does a detail take? Can I wait?
An interior detail is about 3.5–4 hours, a wash & wax 2–3 hours, the combo 6–7. Most jobs are same-day drop-off and pickup rather than a wait.
Why is the price an estimate?
The listed prices cover a normally dirty vehicle. Heavy pet hair, deep stains, or excessive dirt add real time, so we confirm the final number when we see it — no surprises after.
What is paint correction and do I need it?
It’s machine polishing that removes swirl marks and oxidation to bring back gloss. It’s an add-on to a standard detail, and it’s the right first step if you’re planning a ceramic coating.
How often should I have my vehicle detailed?
A full interior once or twice a year keeps ahead of wear; a wash & wax every few months keeps the exterior protected. We can set up a schedule that fits how you drive.
Do you detail boats and jet skis?
Yes — boats, pontoons, and PWCs, priced per foot based on length, condition, and oxidation. Heavy oxidation correction and bilge work are quoted separately.
